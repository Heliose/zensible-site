The thesis

Why An Operating System And Not Another Application

HR technology has spent thirty years perfecting the record. Where someone sits, what they earn, when they joined, which policy applies. The record is excellent.

The work around it is still manual.

An enterprise buying agents today faces two inadequate options. A copilot makes one person faster inside one tool. An agent platform hands a team primitives and wishes them luck. Neither runs operations. Neither holds the context, enforces the policy, or produces the audit trail a regulated function requires.

The missing thing is not a better application. It is the layer applications run on — a runtime, a context graph, a governance spine, an identity model for things that act.

An operating system, in other words. And it has to be built for HR specifically, because the context, policies, compliance surface and lifecycle are domain-shaped.

That knowledge is the product.
