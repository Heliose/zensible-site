05 · Motion

Physical, controlled, responsive.

Motion should explain hierarchy and input—not decorate. Use decisive easing, short feedback transitions, and always respect reduced-motion preferences.

06 · Usage guidance

Confident, never chaotic.

The system works when expressive moments are surrounded by quiet structure. Maintain contrast, hierarchy, and operational clarity at every breakpoint.

Do
  • Use the brand gradient for meaningful emphasis.
  • Keep body copy on simple, high-contrast surfaces.
  • Reserve bright card colors for distinct work categories.
  • Keep the active carousel card upright.
  • Pair motion with keyboard and touch support.
Avoid
  • Using several gradients in one viewport.
  • Placing long copy directly on busy imagery.
  • Mixing unrelated corner-radius styles.
  • Using shadows without a hierarchy purpose.
  • Hiding status meaning in color alone.
